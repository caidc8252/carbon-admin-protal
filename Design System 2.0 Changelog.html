06 Outstanding resolved

App Publish sub-app (app-publish-bundle.jsx) inherits the shell’s data-theme and its token file already carries the DS 2.0 dark + radius values. Root cause of its dark-mode glare was a single undefined alias: var(--bg-surface, #fff) fell back to white in both themes. Fixed by defining --bg-surface → --color-bg-2 (theme-aware) — this cascades to every dropdown, sheet, popover and sticky footer in the bundle at once — plus targeted fixes to the remaining literal #fff surface/hover-reset spots. Remaining: gradient circle avatars (cosmetic, pending ObjectTile swap) and inline numeric radii.
