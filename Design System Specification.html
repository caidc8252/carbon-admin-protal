Dark mode. Linear-influenced: canvas darkest, card one step lighter, sidebar deepest; depth from surface tone + hairlines, not shadow. No light fill, badge, chip, tile, toggle track or row highlight may stay light in dark — every tint has a [data-theme="dark"] value.

Elevation & focus

  • Shadows — 5-step ramp --shadow-1…5. Light = soft drop shadows; dark = near-flat (borders carry definition). Cards use --shadow-1.
  • CTA shadow — --shadow-cta: inset white highlight + 0.5px edge + drop (the signature primary-button lift).
  • Focus ring — --shadow-focus: 3px primary at 25% (light) / 40% (dark). Never removed from focusable controls.
